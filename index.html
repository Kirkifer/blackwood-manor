      What to Test — v0.1

      1. Open GM. Confirm three test investigators are present and the round begins in Action.
      2. Open Player and select a different investigator. Confirm only that investigator's location, stats, and private discoveries are shown.
      3. During Action, choose a neighboring room and submit movement. Try multiple investigators before advancing.
      4. Advance to Discussion. Verify movement resolves and entering an unexplored room creates a new room with an Item, Event, Omen, or no trigger.
      5. Open Public TV. Confirm newly discovered rooms appear, but player positions and private cards do not.
      6. Advance to Haunt. Verify a secret Haunt check happens, then advance back to Action.
      7. Use Force Haunt to test the one-time Haunt Initial Discussion and the public cooperative victory objective.
      8. Test on a narrow phone screen and a wide TV/desktop screen. Check that the map and buttons remain usable.
      9. Refresh the page. Verify the local test state persists on this browser.
      10. Reset Session and confirm everything starts fresh.
      Not in v0.1: live cross-device lobby, real private-data security, QR joining, multiple floors, Speed-length movement paths, combat, stealing, trading, monsters, timers, full haunt objectives, and real win/loss detection. These require the next builds.
